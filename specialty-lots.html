🌾 Empowering the Hands That Harvest

In Ethiopia, women are the backbone of the coffee harvest. They meticulously hand-sort the cherries, manage the washing stations, and drive the quality of the final cup. Yet, they are traditionally underrepresented in leadership and export ownership.

Black Lion intentionally partners with and sources directly from:

  • Women Estate Owners & Smallholders Ensuring premium direct-trade prices go straight into the hands of the female farmers who earn them.
  • Women Q-Graders & Cuppers Utilizing certified female coffee scientists in Addis Ababa to strictly evaluate, score, and verify every single lot we acquire.

When you buy a Black Lion sack, you are investing in a transparent, equitable supply chain.

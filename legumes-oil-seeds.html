Wholesale Product Portfolio & Ordering Specifications

Our agricultural products are meticulously cleaned, double-sorted to eliminate debris, and packed into heavy-duty commercial storage systems designed to preserve optimal oil and moisture levels across long distances.

Premium Split Red Lentils (Bulk Misir)

A critical staple in East African gastronomy that remains notoriously difficult to source at premium quality levels within the United States and international markets. Our red lentils are celebrated for their rapid, uniform cooking time, high protein density, and rich, earthy flavor profile required for authentic Misir Wot.

  • Purity Profile: 99.5% pure split red lentils. Thoroughly machine-cleaned and hand-sorted to ensure zero sand, grit, or foreign stone matter.
  • Bulk Packaging: Multi-wall food-grade sacks with integrated moisture-barrier liners.
  • Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ): 1,000 kg (Metric Ton minimum).

High-Oil Black Cumin Seeds (Tikur Azmud)

Essential for traditional Habesha baking, flavoring clarifying butter, and highly sought after by diaspora communities for natural wellness routines and cold-pressed oil extraction.

  • Purity Profile: 100% natural, unbleached whole black cumin seeds boasting an exceptionally high volatile oil content.
  • Bulk Packaging: Heavy-duty, oxygen-barrier woven polypropylene bags with inner poly-liners.
  • Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ): 500 kg.

Whole Korarima Pods (Black Cardamom)

By exporting korarima exclusively as whole, sun-dried pods, we ensure the tough outer shell locks in the delicate, woody, and minty essential oils. This completely prevents flavor degradation during international shipping.

  • Purity Profile: Grade-A whole dried forest pods. Entirely unbroken, moisture-tested, and free from mold.
  • Bulk Packaging: Vacuum-sealed commercial box cartons.
  • Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ): 250 kg.

Premium Yellow Split Peas (Bulk Kik))

The fundamental base for comforting Kik Alicha stews. These peas are selected for their firm structural integrity, bright natural color, and excellent storage stability.

  • Purity Profile: Double-polished split yellow peas with uniform sizing.
  • Bulk Packaging: Industrial valve woven bags.
  • Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ): 1000 kg.
